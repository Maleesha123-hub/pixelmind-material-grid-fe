## 3. SERVICE PROVIDER RESPONSIBILITIES

In consideration of the monthly service charge, MeskOra Technologies (PVT) Ltd. shall perform the following duties:

* **3.1 Web Infrastructure & Availability:** Maintain the web infrastructure and exercise reasonable efforts to ensure continuous, reliable availability and performance of the web system.
* **3.2 Routine Maintenance & Technical Support:** Carry out routine software maintenance, system health checks, software security patching, bug fixes, and provide standard technical support during regular business hours.
* **3.3 Change Requests (CRs) & Feature Development:** Handle agreed minor change requests, functional refinements, visual adjustments, and minor feature developments within reasonable monthly technical hours.
* **3.4 Disaster Recovery:** Maintain disaster recovery procedures and perform system restoration activities when required to resume web operations after any unforeseen technical event.
* **3.5 System & Database Backups:** Maintain regular, automated database and file backups to safeguard against accidental data loss.
* **3.6 Monitoring & Incident Resolution:** Proactively monitor system availability and resolve reported technical issues within reasonable and prompt timeframes.
* **3.7 Deployed Application Maintenance:** Maintain the deployed production application, SSL security certificates, web server configurations, and underlying software dependencies.

> **Operational Boundary Note:** MeskOra will exercise all standard technical diligence. However, the parties agree that MeskOra does not guarantee 100% uninterrupted uptime or zero external failure, nor does the flat monthly service fee cover unlimited development work or major system overhauls.

## 4. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

To enable smooth delivery and reliable continuous operation, the Client agrees to:

* **4.1 Accurate Requirements & Content:** Promptly provide accurate business specifications, building materials catalogs, product pricing, company information, logos, and digital media required for system configuration.
* **4.2 Timely Feedback & Approvals:** Provide timely feedback, testing sign-offs, and necessary approvals within five (5) business days of submission.
* **4.3 Payment Obligations:** Pay the monthly service charges (LKR 20,000/month) and approved actual infrastructure costs (server hosting and domain registration) promptly as agreed.
* **4.4 Responsible System Use & Credential Protection:** Use the web system responsibly and in compliance with Sri Lankan laws, safeguard administrative login credentials, and prevent unauthorized access.
* **4.5 Issue Notification:** Promptly inform MeskOra of any technical defects, errors, or security concerns encountered during operation.

## 5. PROJECT DELIVERY & SCOPE MANAGEMENT

* **5.1 Delivery & Operational Access:** MeskOra Technologies (PVT) Ltd. will deliver the agreed Building Materials Suppliers web system and provide access credentials to the Client after completing the required setup, deployment, and testing activities.

## 6. OWNERSHIP AND ACCESS RIGHTS

* **6.1 Client Right of Use:** The Client receives a non-exclusive commercial right and license to use the delivered web system for its legitimate business operations.
* **6.2 Service Provider Intellectual Property:** MeskOra Technologies (PVT) Ltd. retains sole and exclusive proprietary ownership of its existing reusable software components, core architectural frameworks, code libraries, utilities, and proprietary development tools used in building or maintaining the system.
* **6.3 Source Code Transfer:** Any transfer or assignment of full source-code ownership to the Client must be agreed upon separately in writing under mutually accepted terms and compensation.
* **6.4 User Credentials:** The Client will receive all necessary administrative logins, passwords, and access permissions required to operate and manage the live system.

---

## 7. HOSTING, DOMAIN AND BACKUPS

* **7.1 Web Server Hosting:** MeskOra will configure and manage the production cloud server. The first-month hosting charge (September 2026) is fixed at LKR 4,700. Subsequent server hosting fees will be billed at actual third-party rates.
* **7.2 Domain Registration & Renewal:** The domain name will be registered and managed by MeskOra on behalf of the Client for one year (September 2026 to September 2027) for LKR 2,300. Future annual renewals will be invoiced to the Client prior to expiration.
* **7.3 Third-Party Outages:** Server hosting and domain registration depend on external cloud service providers. MeskOra shall not be legally liable for temporary disruptions, network routing failures, or downtime caused solely by upstream third-party hosting companies, registrars, or global network failures, but will actively assist in restoring normal operations.
* **7.4 Cost Reimbursement:** Actual server hosting and domain costs are payable directly by the Client or reimbursed to MeskOra Technologies (PVT) Ltd. upon invoice.

## 8. AGREEMENT PERIOD AND TERMINATION

* **8.1 Service Period:** This Agreement covers an agreed monthly service period of six (6) calendar months, from **September 2026 to February 2027** (or five (5) months if confirmed under Section 2).
* **8.2 Renewal & Extension:** After the initial service period, this Agreement may be renewed or continued on a monthly or yearly basis upon mutual written agreement.
* **8.3 Mutual Termination:** Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time by mutual written agreement.
* **8.4 Termination for Material Breach:** Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party commits a material breach of any term (including non-payment) and fails to remedy the breach within fourteen (14) calendar days of receiving written notice.
* **8.5 Payment of Outstanding Fees:** In the event of termination, the Client shall promptly pay all outstanding monthly service fees and approved infrastructure expenses incurred up to the effective termination date.
* **8.6 Handover Arrangement:** Upon termination and full settlement of outstanding fees, MeskOra shall provide an export of the Client's business data and assist in transferring domain administrative controls to the Client.
